Privacy Policy

Effective September 30, 2026

Maceda Lighting respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how we handle information submitted through our website, quote forms, text messaging program, and customer communications.

Information We Collect

We may collect information you provide directly to us, including your name, mobile phone number, email address, property address, requested services, project details, messages, property photos, quote selections, scheduling information, and other information you choose to provide.

How We Use Information

We use this information to respond to quote requests, create custom lighting previews, prepare estimates and proposals, communicate about requested services, schedule and perform installations and removals, provide customer support, process payments, maintain business records, and improve our services.

SMS / Text Messaging Privacy

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Mobile phone numbers, SMS consent, and opt-in information are not sold, rented, transferred, or shared with third parties or affiliates for their own marketing or promotional purposes.

If you opt in to receive text messages from Maceda Lighting, messages may include quote follow-ups, custom lighting previews, package-selection messages, scheduling confirmations, installation or removal reminders, service updates, and other communications related to the service you requested.

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time or HELP for assistance. Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of purchase.

Service Providers

We may use service providers that help us operate our business, such as website hosting, database, payment, image-generation, analytics, and messaging providers. These providers may process information only as needed to perform services for Maceda Lighting. We do not authorize service providers to use your SMS opt-in information for their own marketing or promotional purposes.

Property Photos and Automated Processing

Property photos may be processed by automated tools to create customized lighting visualizations or assist with quote preparation. Generated previews are design concepts and may not exactly match final installation conditions, products, measurements, or placement.

Data Security and Retention

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect customer information. We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide services, maintain business records, resolve disputes, and meet legal or operational requirements.

Your Choices

You may opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP. For help with text messaging, reply HELP or contact Maceda Lighting using the information below.
